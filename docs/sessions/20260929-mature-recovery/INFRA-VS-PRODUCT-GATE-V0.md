# Infrastructure vs product-existence gate v0

This rule applies to the mature-recovery program after restored-intent correction.

## Two independent dimensions

### INFRASTRUCTURE READINESS
Can the product represent identity, evidence, persistence, authority, reproducibility and false-green controls correctly?

### PRODUCT THESIS VALIDATION
Does the repository's distinctive mature thesis work and justify itself against current alternatives?

A repository cannot pass architecture/existence because infrastructure is green.

## Portage
INFRA-01: subject/evidence/Assessment/export machinery.
PRODUCT EXP-P1: semantic task portability across materially different substrate breaking Docker/x86 assumptions.

## PhenoMLX
INFRA-01: RuntimeProfile/Qualification/store/evidence machinery.
PRODUCT EXP-M1: real runtime research mechanism vs best current Unsloth/engine-native alternative, with justified custom work.

## PhenoLab
INFRA-01: Experiment/Candidate/EvaluationReport/Assessment/Decision durability and false-green controls.
PRODUCT EXP-L1: target-driven model+harness research program with multiple interventions, budget/worker continuity and trustworthy success OR failure learning.

## Completion rule
Architecture/product-existence gate requires:
- INFRA dimension sufficient for trustworthy experiment;
- PRODUCT experiment executed;
- alternative stack compared;
- result interpreted without changing acceptance policy post hoc.

If INFRA passes and PRODUCT has not run: status = INFRA_READY_PRODUCT_UNVALIDATED.
If PRODUCT cannot run because INFRA missing: status = INFRA_BLOCKED.
If PRODUCT runs and alternative wins: product thesis may be falsified even though infrastructure is excellent.
